# V0.9.0 acceptance

> Superseded on 2026-11-08: still-relevant items moved to `V0.9.1-ACCEPTANCE.md`, the one open checklist. Kept as history.

V0.9.0 adds themes (twenty-nine built-in templates including Halo and One UI, a theme editor, L-Connect import, palettes), One UI-style touch pages, LED control through OpenRGB with a schedule and display power, weather, aesthetic mode and deeper Plexamp control. Automated checks render pages and themes off-screen and test logic with fixtures; they do not verify behavior on the dedicated displays. Marks below record what was actually confirmed and how.

Legend: **[x] user** = confirmed by the user on their hardware; **[x] live** = exercised against the user's real devices or services by an automated probe with readback (no visual confirmation); **[ ]** = not yet checked.

## Version and upgrade

- [ ] `Pulsewave.exe` file/product version is 0.9.0 and the tray shows V0.9.0.
- [ ] Exit V0.8.0 first. All 12 HWiNFO mappings, the FPS slot, monitor identity, placement, telemetry settings, shortcuts and the Plex token carry over.
- [ ] If Launch with Windows was on, turn it off and on once from the 0.9.0 tray.

## Themes

- [x] user: Halo Column on the HYTE Y70 shows the split clock, weather for the set city, now playing with art in the progress ring, ring gauges, toggles and shortcut tiles (screenshot 2026-11-07, preview build).
- [ ] Halo, One UI and One UI Column apply and read at 3–4 ft; the Halo bar on the 8.8" display.
- [ ] Themes gallery and playlist Show filter (all / fits / horizontal / vertical) and the remembered choice.
- [ ] Colors page and tray → Colors recolor a theme and keep the choice per theme; Theme's own colors restores it.
- [ ] Classic still shows the transparent overlay over Wallpaper Engine.

## Touch pages (click-through off)

- [x] user: media page on the Y70 showed art, controls and the queue (preview build; button overlap and queue scrolling then fixed).
- [ ] Media page after the fix: transport buttons clear of each other on the Y70; queue shows the next 30 tracks; dragging the queue follows the finger and a flick glides; a drag never plays a row.
- [ ] Seek bar drag shows the position while dragging and seeks once on lift.
- [ ] Volume page: dragging a slider changes that app's volume smoothly; tap sets a level; mute works; many apps scroll on the Y70.
- [ ] Lights page brightness drag; profile/effect lists scroll; Open OpenRGB brings OpenRGB forward or starts it.
- [ ] Closing a page with ✕ and tapping where its tiles were does not launch anything.
- [ ] Shortcut tiles: larger rounded, bevelled icons; Steam covers on tall tiles.

## Lights, schedule and display power

- [x] live: OpenRGB SDK commands (profile, color, brightness, effects, off/on) applied to the RTX 5070 Ti and ASUS Z890 with readback (2026-11-07).
- [x] user: schedule screen-off put the Y70 into standby ("it went dark"), and a tap woke it.
- [x] live: DDC/CI turn-on brought the Y70 out of standby with readback (mode 1 after about 5 s).
- [ ] The Y70 comes back on by itself when a screen-off period ends.
- [ ] LEDs turn off and back on with a schedule rule; lights switched by hand in between are left alone.

## Media, weather, aesthetic mode

- [x] live: Plexamp seek, volume, repeat, shuffle and jump-in-queue applied and restored, read back from Plexamp's timeline (2026-11-07).
- [x] live: Open-Meteo city search and forecast (read-only probe).
- [ ] Weather values update every 15 minutes while a weather theme is showing; °F works.
- [ ] Aesthetic mode fades or hides the HUD over the wallpaper; the first tap only wakes it, and it fades again after the wake time.

## Preserved behavior

- [ ] Overlay placement and reconnect recovery, click-through, always-on-top, tray hide/restore, session stats and export, alerts.
- [ ] Settings for removed features remain untouched in settings.json.
