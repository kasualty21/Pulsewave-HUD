## What the automated checks cover

- Frame time: FRAMETIME is RTSS's reading when paired, else 1000 / FPS, blank with neither; the chart scale never drops below its minimum. Halo Gaming (bar and column) is rendered off-screen with a minute of readings: a steady 120 FPS draws no amber, seconds at 20 FPS turn the line amber.
- RTSS: Auto-pair picks "Frame Time" and "Framerate 1% Low" from this PC's HWiNFO export (never the 0.1% low; seconds scaled to ms; wrong units refused). The session 1% low is RTSS's latest reading while playing and the median once saved; without RTSS, or with RTSS reading 0, it is the slowest 1% of seconds as before.
- Shortcut lists: older settings load with none; lists are saved cleaned (known lists, picks in range, no repeats); tiles show a list's picks in order and the drawer shows them first; picks follow their shortcuts through reorder, removal and replacement; Halo Gaming and Productivity (bar and column) follow their lists and Halo Lifestyle the main list. The Shortcuts window was rendered in both modes.
- Audit fixes: the weather symbol follows today's sunrise and sunset; after a game the session shows the last one's length and live FPS is blank; HWiNFO's board-partner name is dropped and themes show "RTX 5070 Ti"; an unexported clock, a turning fan and a media source without a timeline count as nothing to show; drive used of total; wide video thumbnails shown whole.
- All 39 built-in templates pass the quality gate with realistic and worst-case readings; the six Halo templates were rendered and inspected after each layout change.
