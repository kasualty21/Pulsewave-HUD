# V0.8.0 now playing and Plexamp acceptance

> Superseded on 2026-11-08: still-relevant items moved to `V0.9.1-ACCEPTANCE.md`, the one open checklist. Kept as history.

This release replaces system uptime in the Time panel with a now-playing tile and adds Plexamp support. It also changes the target framework (Windows 10 1809 or later) and includes refresh-performance and settings-reload fixes. Automated checks do not verify behavior on the dedicated display; complete the relevant checks with the 0.8.0 executable.

The user has already confirmed Plexamp title, artist and album art on the dedicated display using a preview build of this source.

## Version and upgrade

- [ ] Confirm the executable is `Pulsewave.exe`, its file/product version is 0.8.0, and the tray menu header says V0.8.0.
- [ ] Exit V0.7.1 before first launch. Confirm all 12 HWiNFO mappings, the game-independent FPS slot, monitor identity/scaling, opacity, profiles, telemetry settings and OpenRGB selection remain intact.
- [ ] If Launch with Windows was enabled, confirm it still starts Pulsewave after turning it off and on from the 0.8.0 tray menu.

## Now playing

- [ ] With Spotify or a browser playing, confirm art, ▶ PLAYING, title and artist appear within about two seconds and are legible at 3–4 ft.
- [ ] Pause playback and confirm ❚❚ PAUSED with a dimmed title. Close the player and confirm "Nothing playing".
- [ ] Play a track with a long title and artist; confirm text ends with "…" inside the Time panel and does not overlap the clock or date.
- [ ] Confirm the clock and date are still large and fully visible after moving up.
- [ ] With one app paused and another playing, confirm the playing app is shown.
- [ ] Turn off "Show now playing (replaces uptime)" in the tray; confirm uptime returns and the setting survives a restart. Turn it back on.

## Plexamp

- [x] With a Plex token saved, confirm Plexamp's title, artist and art appear. (User confirmed with a preview build.)
- [ ] Remove the saved token from the tray dialog; confirm the tile shows Plexamp with "Set your Plex token in the tray menu".
- [ ] Re-enter the token and confirm details return without restarting Pulsewave.
- [ ] Confirm `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Pulsewave\settings.json` does not contain the token and `plex-token.dat` is not readable as text.

## Preserved behavior

- [ ] Confirm overlay placement/recovery, click-through, always-on-top, hide/restore, session stats/export and alerts still work.
- [ ] Reload settings.json from the tray while Sensor Manager is open; confirm Sensor Manager closes and the reloaded settings stay in effect.
- [ ] Keep Wallpaper Engine running underneath and confirm transparent overlay behavior and OpenRGB color following remain independent.
- [ ] Open Appearance → Preview current draft; confirm the simulated Time panel shows a sample track instead of uptime.
